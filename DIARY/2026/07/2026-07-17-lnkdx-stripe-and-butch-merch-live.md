# July 17, 2026 — LNKDX becomes a subscription business and Butch's shirts go live

Rich approved the proposed recurring LNKDX Park pricing, then improved it: two-letter and two-number Park names remain premium, while mixed letter-number names cost half as much.

The launch matrix is now:

- Premium founding: $49/year
- Mixed founding: $24.50/year
- Premium standard: $79/year
- Mixed standard: $39.50/year

Regular LinkedIn mirror shortcuts remain free. The paid product is a renewable LNKDX shortcut license, not ownership of a LinkedIn identity. Park checkout uses a transactional availability hold, Stripe-hosted subscription Checkout, verified webhooks, automated activation, annual renewal state, and a grace period for failed or canceled subscriptions.

During setup, a live Stripe secret was pasted into chat. The key was never written into project files. It must be rolled and replaced through Firebase Secret Manager before launch.

The same patch records a happy milestone for The Butch Breger Museum: all three submitted shirts were approved by Amazon Merch on Demand. Birthday Donkey, Movie Star Dog, and Mirror Pig now have their direct ASIN destinations, and the complete Museum brand page becomes a fourth ShareShuffle ad.
