# July 20, 2026 — Before the credits, there is the work

Charlie R. Williams needed a professional home before he had a professional credit list. That is not a contradiction. Every actor has a first reel, a first audition, and a first day when the work is real even though the résumé is still short.

Patch 20 adds `CharlieRW.com`, a one-page voiceover portfolio built around that honest beginning. Charlie's experience backstage as a student lighting director becomes the opening chapter: he learned to notice timing, tone, and how small choices change a scene. His mentorship with Joe Loesch and his first professional demo session provide the next chapter.

The site does not invent credits. It positions Charlie through his point of view, vocal qualities, preparation, and three scripts ready for the demo session: commercial, explainer, and animation. Until the finished audio arrives, the reel player deliberately reports that the demo is in production. One configuration file can activate the finished reel and résumé without redesigning the page.

This is also the first practical example of a Tempo idea becoming a reusable professional identity: a memorable domain, a focused story, clean media links, and a parent-managed contact path.

## Patch 21 — Charlie chooses the pictures

The first release used polished studio-style headshots. Charlie chose a different introduction: four candid photos that actually feel like him. Patch 21 puts his white-shirt portrait at the top, lets a colorful dinosaur share the story section, and turns the gallery into three honest moods—focused, in character, and out in the wild.

The visual change supports the premise already in the writing: the interesting thing is not manufactured polish, but a real point of view. New cache-safe filenames make the replacement visible immediately after deployment, and every published copy is resized and stripped of embedded metadata.
