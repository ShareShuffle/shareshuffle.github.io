## Butch Breger merchandise

Three original drawings were submitted to Amazon Merch on Demand:

- Birthday Donkey
- Movie Star Dog
- Mirror Pig

The Amazon brand was set to:

`The Ernest Lee "Butch The Barn Man" Breger Museum`

Rules established:

- preserve Butch’s original linework, wording, spelling, and paper-background presentation;
- do not invent prices, ratings, ASINs, inventory, shipping claims, reviews, or Amazon imagery;
- pending cards should say Amazon review is in progress;
- central product records should allow one destination update after approval;
- physical ownership of artwork is not automatically the same as copyright ownership, so rights provenance must remain documented and honest.
