Quick answers

What does ShareShuffle save?Product URLs, titles, images, notes you write, and optional shelf information used to create share links and collections.
Can links include affiliate tracking?Yes. Some links may be affiliate links. As an Amazon Associate, ShareShuffle earns from qualifying purchases.
Do I need an account to open a share?No. Shared pages and public shelves are designed to open without requiring an account.
What is ShelfMix?ShelfMix is the collection layer for ShareShuffle — a place to gather related recommendations into reusable shelves.
