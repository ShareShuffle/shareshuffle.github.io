ShareShuffle Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

ShareShuffle helps users save, organize, and share product recommendations from websites they visit.

Information We Collect

ShareShuffle may collect and store:

  • Product page URLs selected by the user
  • Product titles selected by the user
  • Product images available on the selected page
  • User-entered notes and shelf names
  • Generated share links
  • Support requests submitted through our website
  • Email addresses voluntarily provided through support or waitlist forms

ShareShuffle does not collect passwords, payment information, health information, government identification numbers, or precise location data.

How Information Is Used

  • Create recommendation links
  • Organize recommendations into shelves and collections
  • Display recommendation pages
  • Track share usage statistics
  • Improve ShareShuffle features
  • Respond to support requests

Data Sharing

ShareShuffle does not sell user information.

ShareShuffle does not rent user information.

ShareShuffle may share information with trusted service providers such as cloud hosting and database providers solely to operate the service.

Chrome Extension Permissions

The ShareShuffle Chrome Extension may use the following permissions:

  • activeTab — access the page the user chooses to share
  • tabs — identify the current page and display extension status
  • scripting — retrieve page information such as titles, images, and URLs
  • clipboardWrite — copy generated share links when requested
  • storage — remember recent shelves, notes, and links
  • host permissions — access supported websites so recommendations can be created

User Content

Users are responsible for any notes, shelf names, and recommendation content they choose to save or share.

Data Retention

Information may be retained to provide ongoing service functionality, maintain share pages, and improve the platform.

Users may request removal of support submissions by contacting us.

Children's Privacy

ShareShuffle is not directed toward children under 13 years of age.

Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy may be sent to:

support@shareshuffle.com

Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
