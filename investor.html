Brand / investor notes

The shelf is the product.

ShareShuffle is a low-friction recommendation layer for commerce: people share trusted shelves and individual product links; ShareShuffle owns the destination, analytics, and affiliate monetization.

Initial wedge

Bass players and musicians

Rich’s Bass Gear, Worship Bass Essentials, Church Tech Shelf, Recording Gear Shelf.

Revenue

Affiliate-first

Platform owner receives affiliate revenue. No creator commissions at launch, reducing spam incentives.

Trust

Visible, not weaponized

Notes and titles are sanitized so gambling, spam, and executable-looking URLs are visible but not clickable.

Brand architecture

ShareShuffle

Company/product brand and Chrome extension.

SHFL.ME

Transport layer and short-link domain.

ShelfMix

Destination brand for public but unlisted recommendation shelves.
