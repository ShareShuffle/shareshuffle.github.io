Official brand standards

Share what’s worth finding.

ShareShuffle turns scattered recommendations into trusted shelves, clean short links, and product pages with context. The brand should feel human, fast, colorful, useful, and credible enough for both customers and investors.

See the product story Investor overview

Brand idea

ShareShuffle is a recommendation layer for real human taste: shelves, notes, short links, product cards, and context.

Core positioning

For customers

A better way to save and share the things you recommend all the time.

For investors

A lightweight commerce layer that owns the destination, the analytics, and affiliate monetization.

For creators

A public but controlled shelf of trusted picks without becoming a social feed.

For brands

A cleaner path from recommendation to purchase, powered by context instead of noise.

Messaging

Voice and pitch language

Use this language

  • Share what’s worth finding.
  • Stop sharing products. Share your shelf.
  • Recommendations with context.
  • Trusted picks, organized into shelves.
  • Short links that lead to richer product pages.
  • A commerce layer built around real human taste.
  • Public shelves, private intent, clean sharing.

Avoid this language

  • Do not position it as another social network.
  • Do not over-promise AI recommendations.
  • Do not make it sound like spammy affiliate marketing.
  • Do not lead with memberships or creator commissions.
  • Do not hide that commerce is part of the model.
  • Do not make the shelf feel secondary.
Master pitch

ShareShuffle turns recommendations into destinations.

People already recommend products every day through text, email, church groups, bands, families, group chats, and creator communities. ShareShuffle gives those recommendations a better container: a short link, a note, a shelf, a product card, and a path to revenue.

Why it matters

The shelf becomes the reusable asset. The share link becomes the transport layer. ShelfMix becomes the destination.
