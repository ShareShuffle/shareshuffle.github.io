Clear purpose and limited access

Why do our applications use Google Sign-In?

Some Tempo Foundry applications let people create accounts to save and manage their own information. Google Sign-In provides secure authentication without requiring Tempo Foundry to create or store another password.

  • Identify returning users and attach saved content to the correct account.
  • Protect private dashboards and prevent unauthorized access.
  • Let users synchronize and edit their own saved links, settings, recommendations, or product content.
  • Reduce password-related security risks.
What we receive

For ordinary authentication, we may receive a Google account identifier, name, email address, profile image, and verification status. Tempo Foundry never receives or stores your Google password. Products request only the access needed for the feature being used.

About the studio

Founded by Rich Williams.
