# Gracefeed / Tempo Foundry

Gracefeed is the company identity behind Tempo Foundry. Tempo Foundry is the
public studio and portfolio brand for software and preservation projects that
help people discover things worth sharing and preserve things worth remembering.

The exact registered legal name and suffix still need to be confirmed before
legal documents, contracts, invoices, or public footer language are changed.

We believe software should feel welcoming, understandable, authentic, and built to last.
