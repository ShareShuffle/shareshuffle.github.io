About Douglas

A performer’s instinct for the page.

Douglas N. Wilkinson is an actor and screenwriter drawn to grounded people facing extraordinary pressure. His work favors tension with a human pulse: ordinary communities, buried decisions, and characters who have to choose what they are willing to risk.

His feature thriller On Fractured Ground has earned two 2026 screenplay competition semi-finalist honors.
