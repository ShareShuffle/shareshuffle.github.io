Charlie's story

Before the credits, there is the work.

Charlie learned early that performance is built from details. As the lighting director for a school production, he watched timing, tone, and one small creative choice change the feeling of an entire scene.

Voiceover gave him a new place to use that instinct. He listens for what a script is actually trying to say, then looks for the most believable way to say it. No borrowed credits. No manufactured mythology. Just a young performer learning the craft and doing the work correctly from the beginning.

His first professional demo is not a retrospective. It is the opening scene.

Mentorship Developing his voice with mentor Joe Loesch
What Charlie brings

A voice that sounds like somebody.

The goal is not to sound like an announcer. It is to sound like the person the listener believes—whether that person is explaining, recommending, wondering, or arguing with an off-screen dad.

Conversational

Relaxed, believable delivery that treats copy like a real thought.

Thoughtful

Clear intelligence and curiosity without sounding overly polished.

Dryly funny

Comic timing that trusts the writing instead of elbowing the joke.

Exploratory

Willing to try the unexpected read and adjust as the direction evolves.
