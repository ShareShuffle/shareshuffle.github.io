A paid Park subscription grants a limited, renewable, nonexclusive license to use one available two-character LNKDX shortcut while the subscription remains active. Pricing and renewal

Cancellation, failed renewal, and release

You may cancel renewal through Stripe’s customer portal. A payment failure or canceled subscription may enter a 14-day grace period. After the grace period, the shortcut may stop redirecting and may eventually become available to another customer. Canceling does not transfer ownership of the shortcut.

Refunds

Because a scarce shortcut is reserved and provisioned immediately after payment, fees are generally nonrefundable once activated, except where required by law or when LNKDX cannot provide the purchased shortcut. Duplicate or erroneous charges will be reviewed in good faith using the contact information shown on the Stripe receipt.

The service is provided on an as-available basis. LNKDX may change technical implementation, prevent unsafe redirects, or discontinue a shortcut when necessary.
