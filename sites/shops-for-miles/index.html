The campus

28 shops around the perimeter. Plenty of room in the middle.

The concept places private shop buildings around the outside of the property, leaving a broad internal drive for circulation, loading, trailers, and easy access.

  • Approximately ten feet between neighboring buildings
  • Seven groups of four buildings sharing a restroom facility
  • Two garage bays and one office in every shop
  • Separate public business listings for every tenant
