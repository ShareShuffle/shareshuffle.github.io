Solution Workshop respects your privacy. We collect only the information reasonably needed to respond to inquiries, support customers, coordinate appointments and projects, and operate our websites and communications.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to Solution Workshop websites, estimate and contact forms, phone calls, emails, text messages, and the administrative or customer-communication services that Solution Workshop provides for the service brands it operates or supports.

When another company or service professional is identified as the provider responsible for a project, that provider may have its own privacy practices. We encourage you to review any policy presented with the estimate, work order, or booking.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect:

  • Contact details: your name, email address, telephone number, service address, billing address, and preferred method of contact.
  • Service and project details: descriptions, photographs, fixture or equipment preferences, scheduling availability, access instructions, estimates, work orders, and related customer requests.
  • Communication history: emails, text messages, form submissions, appointment details, support requests, and notes from telephone conversations. Calls may be monitored or recorded only where permitted by law and when appropriate notice is provided.
  • Transaction information: invoice status, payment amount, and limited transaction details. If online payments are offered, payment-card data is processed by the payment provider and is not intended to be stored by Solution Workshop.
  • Website and device information: internet protocol address, browser and device type, referring page, pages viewed, approximate location derived from an IP address, and diagnostic or security logs.

We may receive this information directly from you, from someone authorized to act for you, from a service professional assigned to your request, or automatically through your use of the website.

3. How we use information

We use personal information to:

  • respond to questions and process estimate or service requests;
  • schedule, confirm, update, and complete appointments or projects;
  • send technician arrival windows, delay notices, project updates, invoices, and requested support;
  • coordinate with the service brand, contractor, technician, vendor, or professional responsible for the work;
  • maintain records, improve our workflows and customer experience, and prevent fraud or misuse;
  • operate, secure, troubleshoot, and measure our websites and communications; and
  • comply with law, enforce agreements, and protect our customers, personnel, and business.

We do not use automated decision-making to make decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects about customers.

4. Mobile information and SMS policy

No sale or marketing disclosure of mobile data. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.
  • Consent: We send text messages only when you voluntarily opt in, initiate a text conversation, ask us to text you, or provide consent through a service call or web form. Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of purchase.
  • Purpose: Messages are transactional and conversational, such as estimate notices, appointment scheduling, arrival-window confirmations, technician delays, project questions, and customer support.
  • Frequency: Message frequency varies based on your active service request, appointment schedule, and replies. We do not use this program for recurring marketing messages.
  • Charges: Message and data rates may apply according to your wireless plan.
  • Opt out: Reply STOP to any message to unsubscribe. We may send one final message confirming the opt-out. You may later opt in again by contacting us or starting a new text conversation.
  • Help: Reply HELP, call or text (214) 945‑3733, or email support@solwx.com.

5. How we disclose information

We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information only as reasonably necessary to:

  • the service brand, contractor, technician, or vendor assigned to fulfill your request;
  • vendors that provide hosting, communications, scheduling, payment processing, security, analytics, or business operations on our behalf and under appropriate restrictions;
  • government authorities, insurers, attorneys, or other parties when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, property, or prevent fraud; and
  • a successor in a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, or sale of business assets, subject to applicable law.

The mobile-information and opt-in restrictions in Section 4 apply regardless of the other disclosure categories in this policy.

6. Cookies and site data

Our website may use essential cookies or similar technologies for security, preferences, performance, and basic analytics. You can restrict cookies in your browser settings, although doing so may affect some site features. We do not knowingly use sensitive personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

7. Data retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain project and financial records, resolve disputes, meet warranty or insurance needs, comply with law, and protect the integrity of our systems. Retention periods vary according to the type of record and the reason it was collected. We delete or de-identify information when it is no longer reasonably needed, subject to legal and operational requirements.

8. Data protection and security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect information against loss, misuse, unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. No website, network, or method of storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Please do not send payment-card numbers, government identification numbers, passwords, medical information, or other sensitive information through ordinary email, text messages, or open website fields unless specifically requested through a secure channel.

9. Your choices

You may ask to access, correct, or delete personal information maintained by Solution Workshop, or update your communication preferences, by contacting us. We will evaluate requests under applicable law and may need to verify your identity. We may retain information when required for legal, accounting, safety, fraud-prevention, or contractual purposes.

You may opt out of email communications by using any unsubscribe method provided or contacting us. For text messages, reply STOP.

10. Children’s privacy

Our websites and services are intended for adults and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information to us, please contact us so we can review and, where appropriate, delete it.

11. Third-party services and links

Our website or communications may link to third-party websites, payment providers, scheduling tools, or service-provider systems. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, and Solution Workshop is not responsible for those independent practices.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our services, technology, or legal obligations change. The effective date at the top identifies the current version. Material changes will be posted on this page and, when appropriate, communicated through another reasonable method.
