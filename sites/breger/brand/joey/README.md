# Tempo Joey Brand System 1.0

Joey is the **female Tempo Foundry mascot**. Her pronouns are **she/her**.

## Official variants

### Mark Joey
The original simplified face. Use for favicons, browser tabs, Apple touch icons, small navigation, and compact Tempo Foundry credits.

### Character Joey
The expressive white-eyed Joey on a yellow badge. Use for Tempo Foundry heroes, Google OAuth, account pages, and large welcome moments.

### Hello Joey
Use for greetings, empty states, success states, and friendly support messages.

### Pointing Joey
Use for calls to action, tips, next steps, and “try this” guidance.

### Teacher Joey
Use for LinkWombat teacher login, invitations, educator onboarding, and teacher-focused help.

### Wide Joey
Use for LinkWombat marketing hero sections.

## Non-negotiable art rules

- Joey’s eye whites and highlights are opaque. Background colors must not bleed through them.
- The cream forelock is her signature recognition feature.
- Keep her tan/cream fur, black outline, friendly expression, and warm pink ears.
- Do not stretch, squash, recolor, or add random costumes.
- Joey supports each product; she does not replace the product’s own identity.

## Product strength

- **Heavy:** LinkWombat
- **Medium:** Tempo Foundry and account/OAuth pages
- **Light:** ShareShuffle, LNKDX, Duet Loop, Metromance, The Butch Breger Museum, Eternal Route 66, and Charlie RW
