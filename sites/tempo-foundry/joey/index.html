The Tempo Foundry mascot

Meet Joey.

Joey is the friendly female wombat who helps connect the Tempo Foundry family. You’ll see her welcoming people, pointing out useful next steps, celebrating finished work, and quietly reminding visitors that very different projects were built with the same care.

She is curious, helpful, joyful, and always ready to lend a paw.

Her role in the studio

Joey supports the products. She doesn’t replace them.

LinkWombat uses Joey heavily. Tempo Foundry and shared account pages use her at medium strength. The museum, Route 66, Charlie RW, and other product brands use a light studio credit so their own identities remain primary.

The eye rule

Joey’s eye whites and highlights are fully opaque. They stay white on yellow, blue, dark mode, print, stickers, and every other approved background.
