Featured project

On Fractured Ground

Some truths aren’t meant to be buried.

When strange tremors and unexplained illnesses strike a suburban neighborhood, retired engineer Tex and his reclusive neighbor follow the evidence from contaminated water to a corporate plan engineered to profit from catastrophe.

The feature screenplay grew from an unpublished novel manuscript by Richard C. Williams. Douglas N. Wilkinson carried forward its characters, locale, and initiating situations, then rebuilt the material into a substantially new screen story.

The writer

Douglas N. Wilkinson

Douglas Wilkinson is an actor and screenwriter developing feature stories grounded in pressure, consequence, and moral choice. His public slate moves from present-day conspiracy and mystery to the hard edge of the American West, always returning to characters forced to decide who they will become when the world stops giving them easy answers.

Five projects are currently ready for public consideration and optioning. Douglas is open to representation, producing relationships, and conversations with serious creative collaborators.
