E‑Town

In a town beyond the reach of law, justice becomes a choice.

View ScriptHop packet Page Turner listing
Based on true eventsE‑TownWritten by Douglas N. Wilkinson
The story

Elizabethtown, 1870. The law arrives slowly.

A notorious gunfighter protects the sole survivor of a brutal killer, but when the law fails, her courage pushes him to choose between legal justice and the justice no one else can deliver.

Based on true events in New Mexico Territory, E‑Town unfolds in Elizabethtown at the edge of wilderness and law. After surviving a brutal attack, Rosa refuses to disappear while Charles Kennedy—the killer who turned the mountain pass into a hunting ground—remains a threat.

Her defiance draws notorious gunfighter Clay Allison into a conflict that reaches beyond protection. Haunted by the violence he could not stop as a child, Clay must decide whether to preserve the fragile peace of a frightened town or deliver the justice its institutions cannot.
