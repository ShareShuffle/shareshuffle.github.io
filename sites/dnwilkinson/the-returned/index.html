ReturnedWritten by Douglas N. Wilkinson
The story

She came home ten years older.

When his missing daughter returns, a father's search for answers unearths a truth far stranger and more dangerous than he ever could have imagined.

The Rider family’s quiet camping trip fractures when six-year-old Emily disappears without tracks or witnesses. Hours later, police bring back someone unmistakably Emily—terrified, carrying the same memories, and now sixteen years old.

The Briarcroft Research Institute moves quickly to place her under observation. As Emily reacts violently to the facility’s machines, her family discovers other children who vanished, returned older, and were absorbed into programs that officially do not exist. Their search for answers becomes a struggle against secrecy, agencies, and a system more interested in what Emily knows than in giving her back.

Recognition

The Returned won Best Screenplay in the November 2025 Wiki Screenplay Awards and received a Commendable Qualities review in the 2025 Final Draft Big Break Screenwriting Contest.

2025: Wiki Screenplay Awards — Winner, Best Screenplay · Final Draft Big Break Screenwriting Contest — Commendable Qualities review

The dramatic engine

At its center, The Returned is about parenthood under impossible conditions: loving the child who came back while grieving the years—and the version of her—that disappeared. The mystery expands the stakes, but the family’s choice to trust Emily over every official answer keeps the story human.
