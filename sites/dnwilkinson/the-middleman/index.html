The Middleman

The only way out is to become the man he was meant to be.

A feature screenplayThe
MiddlemanWritten by Douglas N. Wilkinson
The story

Every choice reveals a direction.

After a violent attack, a directionless young man is thrust into a mysterious spiritual trial where every choice pulls him toward darkness or grace, and the only escape is becoming the man he was meant to be.

The Middleman moves through the uncertain territory between trauma and transformation. Its protagonist cannot return to the life he was drifting through; the trial in front of him turns each decision into evidence of the person he is choosing to become.

Rather than treating good and evil as abstractions, the screenplay makes them immediate, cumulative, and personal. The central question is not simply whether he survives the trial, but whether survival without change would count as escape at all.

The dramatic engine

A violent rupture forces an unformed young man to confront consequence in a world where his choices carry visible moral weight. The concept combines an internal coming-of-age journey with the forward momentum of a high-stakes trial.
