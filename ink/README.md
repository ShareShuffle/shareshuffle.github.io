## Publishing rules

- Credit tattooers and studios exactly as approved.
- Distinguish the tattooer from the designer, photographer, and owner when those
  are different people.
- Obtain permission before linking personal profiles or publishing appointment
  details.
- Do not imply that a studio still operates at a historical location without
  checking.
- Add photographs of the tattoos separately from transparent illustration assets.
- Keep accessibility descriptions factual and visual; put personal stories in a
  separate caption field.
