Not a social network. Not a shopping site.

ShareShuffle is recommendation infrastructure for clean share links, curated shelves, Amazon affiliate-friendly product pages, and lightweight tools for musicians, gear nerds, church tech teams, creators, and people who send “you should check this out” links.

SHFL.ME

Short links

Readable share links for individual products and shelves.

Shelves

Share-a-shelf collections

Recommendation pages like Worship Bass Essentials, Recording Gear Shelf, and Church Tech Shelf.

Extension

Fast capture

The Chrome extension grabs title, image, URL, note, shelf, and generates a shareable link.

Share Rescue

Send the thing you meant to send.

A copied URL is just an address. ShareShuffle adds the missing layer: intent, context, cleaner previews, and a personal note so the recipient understands why you sent it.

Search Result Rescue

Catch search links before they confuse people

If you paste an Amazon, YouTube, Google, Walmart, Target, eBay, or Best Buy search URL, ShareShuffle now explains that search results are not the same as a specific product, video, or page.

Image Rescue

Pick the best image

When a page has multiple possible images, the app can show choices so your share card looks intentional instead of broken, blank, or weirdly cropped.

Message Ready

Copy text, share sheet, SMS, or email

ShareShuffle prepares the link and the message around it, so you can send a recommendation instead of dumping a raw URL into a chat.

Real referrals

Affiliate-aware, not affiliate-first

When a recommendation is for a participating retailer, links may include affiliate tracking with clear disclosure. The goal is still a useful, honest recommendation.

From messy link to clear recommendation.

ShareShuffle keeps improving the old copy-and-paste routine: clean short links, shelves, preview images, personal notes, generated share cards, and gentle warnings when the link looks like a search page instead of the exact thing you meant.
